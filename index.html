    The Agency

    A gallery without walls,
    or mystery.

    Atelier Nova is an independent art brokerage and curation structure based in Barcelona.

    Designed to break away from the opaque and traditional codes of classic galleries, the agency connects high-potential emerging artists with a new generation of collectors, young entrepreneurs, and senior executives looking for unique pieces for their interiors and professional spaces.

    Our Mission

    Eliminating barriers
    in the art market.

    Offering rigorous, transparent, and agile curation. A 100% digital model, without inventory or heavy overhead, ensuring fluid, exclusive, and bespoke acquisitions between creators and buyers across Europe.

    • 01

      Rigorous

      Every artwork is carefully chosen, not just listed. We champion a distinct vision—a demanding curation that spots high-potential emerging artists.

    • 02

      Transparent

      No codes reserved for insiders. Artworks, terms, and acquisition processes are explained simply before you commit.

    • 03

      Agile

      100% digital, with no stock or heavy overhead. Fluid, custom-tailored acquisitions built for the lifestyle of both buyers and creators.

    Our Story

    Two worlds that never used to cross paths.

    On one side, emerging artists with unique work who struggle to find buyers outside traditional circuits. On the other, young art lovers and young entrepreneurs who want to bring artworks into their daily lives, but feel intimidated by classic galleries.

    Atelier Nova was born in Barcelona to bridge these two worlds. With no storefront to maintain and no inventory to tie down, we put all our energy into what matters most: selecting, explaining, connecting.

    Today, we support the artists who join us and guide every new buyer, from their first spark of inspiration to the piece hanging on their wall at home or in the office.
